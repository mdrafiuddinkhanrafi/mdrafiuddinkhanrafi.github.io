About Me

Engineering-driven SOC Analyst

I am Md Rafiuddin Khan Rafi — a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at Green University of Bangladesh and a dedicated SOC Analyst & Defensive Cybersecurity Specialist (known in the security ecosystem as mdrafi991ex). My core practice is anchored in SIEM alert monitoring, threat detection, incident triage, log analysis, and network packet visibility.

What elevates my defensive capabilities is my engineering-first approach: I do not treat systems as black boxes. Whether fine-tuning Wazuh SIEM rules, managing automated incident response cases in TheHive & Cortex, conducting AI-assisted verification (certified in Software Testing with Generative AI), or coordinating team action during high-tempo operations (certified in Leadership & Crisis Management), I bridge raw defensive operations with robust software engineering to build resilient, uncompromised architectures.
